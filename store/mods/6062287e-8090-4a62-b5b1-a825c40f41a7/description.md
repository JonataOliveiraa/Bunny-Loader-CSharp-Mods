**Archery Overhaul** for **Bunny Loader**. A port of the tModLoader mod created by
**Riptide** (1.4.5 port, fixes and config by **MAR**).

## What's inside

- 50+ bows, 6 crossbows with a charged shot (right-click, or the crossbow button on mobile), 25 arrows, 5 arrow grenades
- 8 quivers that show on the player's back (with or without the bow)
- Arrow bags and jungle/ice bags that drop from enemies as you progress
- 4 armor sets, 30 accessories, 15 materials, steel tools, a potion
- Archer NPC with a shop, Zombie Archer enemy, new prefixes
- Arrow crits create a splash-damage shockwave
- Impact effects, sounds, arrow gores and reusable arrows
- Auto-swing for ranged weapons, alternate recipes and config options
