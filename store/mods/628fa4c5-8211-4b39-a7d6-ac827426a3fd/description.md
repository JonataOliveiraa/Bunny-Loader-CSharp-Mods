# Umamusume Vanities

Free, non-commercial fan port of Umamusume Vanities for Terraria on Android
(Bunny Loader), based on Nyacro's original PC mod.

Features 43 vanity items, character outfits and faces, animated hair and tails,
the Mambo pet, individual running trails and special effects, plus settings for
visual quality and effect quantity (0-200%). Texts in Brazilian Portuguese and
English.
