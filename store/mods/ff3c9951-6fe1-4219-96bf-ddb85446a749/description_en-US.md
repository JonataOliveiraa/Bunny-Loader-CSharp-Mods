# 🌙 Mount and Journey FM

**🐉 Extraordinary mounts · ✨ New forms · 🌌 A journey beyond the ordinary**

---

> **Terraria Mobile port** · Bunny Loader · Made with the original author's permission (Qz)

**Your next adventure begins when you find a new way to explore the world.**

**Mount and Journey FM** brings the mod *Mount and Journey*, created by **Qz**, to **Terraria Mobile**, with its scripts adapted for **Bunny Loader**.

## ✨ Highlights

- 🐉 **Mounts** for every kind of terrain: raptors, spiders, dragons, wyverns, the pillar ships and much more.
- 🦊 **Transformations** into animals and creatures.
- 🧿 **Accessories** that improve any mount and can be combined.
- ⚡ **Weather Orb**, which awakens the Mythical Dragon and unleashes lightning.
- 🎮 **Touch controls** for dashes, teleporting, aiming and discharges.

## 🚀 Getting started

1. Get a mount or transformation item through a recipe, a drop, fishing or the Extractinator.
2. Use the item to summon the mount or transform.
3. Equip accessories to gain speed, defense, flight and dash power.
4. Turn on the touch buttons in the game's controls editor for mounts that have special actions.
