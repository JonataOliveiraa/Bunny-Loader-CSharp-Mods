# Begone, Evil!

**Disable Evil Biome Spread** stops the natural spread of **Corruption**, **Crimson** and **Hallow** without changing the world's normal progression.

> [!TIP]
> Spreading is **blocked by default** as soon as the mod is active. Use `/spread` in chat to allow or block it again in that world.

## What the mod does

- Blocks the natural spread of **Corruption**, **Crimson** and **Hallow**.
- Saves the setting **separately for each world**.
- Keeps the state in sync in **multiplayer**.
- Keeps the normal generation of the Hardmode biomes when the Wall of Flesh is defeated.
- Works the same way as the Journey Mode **Infection Spread** toggle.

## Command

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/spread` | Toggles the spread of the evil biomes and the Hallow in the current world. |

> [!NOTE]
> Even with spreading blocked, you can still see Corrupt, Crimson or Hallow grass advancing over dirt, and a few nearby walls changing along with blocks that were already converted. This also happens with the Journey Mode toggle. What stays blocked is the normal conversion of blocks and healthy grass that really makes the biome spread.

## Compatibility

The mod does no heavy world scans. The block happens directly during Terraria's spread updates, which keeps the vanilla behavior when `/spread` allows spreading again.
