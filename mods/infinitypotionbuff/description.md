# Infinity Potion Buff

Keep items in your Piggy Bank to get their buffs for as long as they stay there:

- a stack of 30 or more of any potion;
- buff stations and furniture, such as the Crystal Ball, Ammo Box and Bewitching Table;
- environment items, such as the Campfire, Heart Lantern, Sunflower and Star in a Bottle;
- any kite, which gives Windy Day.
