## 2.0.0

Ported from JavaScript to C#. The Preview config button is gone because C# configs have no buttons: use /npcannounce preview. The s and % suffixes on sliders and the greyed-out Max Per NPC row are gone too, because the C# config screen has no such options. The banner's enter, show and exit times and the client's start-up wait are measured in real time instead of counted frames, so they keep their length at any frame rate.
