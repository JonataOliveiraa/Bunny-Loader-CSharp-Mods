## 2.0.0

Ported from JavaScript to C#. The config header has no custom color and the quantity has no % suffix, because C# configs have neither. The permission Nyacro gave the JavaScript porter does not extend to this port, and none is claimed.

## 1.0

Umamusume Vanities arrives on Terraria Android.

- **43 vanity items:** character outfits, hair, ears, tails and accessories.
- **Animated visuals:** original faces, moving hair and swaying tails.
- **Running:** unique trails, Sonic Boom and each character's special effects, including Oguri Cap's afterimage and Gold Ship's anchor.
- **Mambo pet** and original sound effects.
- **Visual settings:** Low, Balanced, High or Cinematic quality, effect quantity from 0% to 200% and extra glow.
- **Languages:** Brazilian Portuguese and English.
- **Credits:** Nyacro (original mod), Cygames (characters) and Mantovani (Android port).
